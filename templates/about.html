Context, not just keywords.

This project uses a fine-tuned BERT-based model to classify text as hate speech or non-hate speech. By learning context from a large corpus and then adapting to labelled examples, the model can look beyond isolated terms.

Use the analysis desk to test written text or upload supported audio and video. The goal is a more careful first signal for moderation workflows and a safer digital world.
